How to use the Recipe Document Assistant

Ask questions about Middle Eastern recipes from our collection of authentic cookbooks.

Example queries:

  • "How do I make hummus?"
  • "Show me vegetarian recipes"
  • "What's a traditional dessert from Lebanon?"
  • "Find recipes with eggplant"

Quick Tools:

Use the buttons at the bottom to quickly find recipes by category.

Note:

The assistant only answers based on the available recipe documents. If a recipe isn't in our collection, it won't be able to help.
